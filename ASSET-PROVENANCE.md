# Asset Provenance and Rights Gate

This production package uses a strict rights-first asset policy.

## Approved source classes
1. Original/generated GXEON artwork created specifically for this package.
2. Captures of public RustChain source/specification used for technical explanation with attribution and links in SOURCES.md.
3. User-controlled assets only when ownership or publication rights are known.

## Drive asset audit
A connected Drive contains a large thumbnail-design library including backgrounds, gradients, lights, textures, editable thumbnails and image effects. Some items are marked as shared and their redistribution license is not established by the available metadata.

Therefore those files are **not copied into this public bounty repository** and are not treated as publication-ready assets until rights are verified. They may be used only as private visual references.

## Publication rule
No asset with uncertain rights is shipped in the final video, thumbnails, or public package. This keeps the Type A kit reproducible and rights-safe.
