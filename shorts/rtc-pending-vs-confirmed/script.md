# RTC payment states — short narration

0–8 seconds: Bounty accepted? That does not automatically mean tokens are settled.

8–20 seconds: A maintainer may identify a payout as pending, with an identifier and transaction reference.

20–34 seconds: Check the wallet history and wait for confirmation. Do not confuse pending with confirmed.

34–46 seconds: Keep two separate columns for pending rewards and confirmed receipts. Never count a transfer twice.

46–55 seconds: RTC is experimental. There is no official cash conversion or guaranteed value.

This is an original script, not a live transaction demonstration.
